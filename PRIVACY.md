# Latestwise privacy policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Latestwise performs document checks locally on your Windows computer. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

## Information processed locally

When you choose a document, Latestwise may process its filename, path, file type, size, modification information, bounded document content, structure, and a cryptographic fingerprint. The same local evidence may be examined for files inside folders you explicitly approve.

This information is used only to identify related copies and explain the comparison result.

## Saved settings

Latestwise stores your appearance preference and approved folder paths in:

`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Latestwise\settings.json`

Document contents and comparison results are not uploaded by the application.

## Information not collected

Latestwise does not:

- Require an online account
- Upload documents or document information
- Include advertising or analytics
- Perform background folder scanning
- Access folders you have not approved
- Automatically download online-only cloud files
- Replace, rename, move, or delete documents

## Network access

The application does not require an internet connection for document comparison. Distribution platforms may handle installation and updates independently of the application.

## Data removal

Remove approved folders from the application to stop them from being checked. Close Latestwise and delete `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Latestwise\settings.json` to remove saved application preferences.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the [Latestwise repository](https://github.com/KSAGlory/Latestwise/issues) or contact the KSAGlory community at [discord.gg/ksahub](https://discord.gg/ksahub).
